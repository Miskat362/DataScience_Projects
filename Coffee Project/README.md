# ☕ Coffee Production Analysis

Welcome to the **Coffee Production Analysis** project! This repository explores global coffee production, pricing, and consumption trends using Python, pandas, and Matplotlib. Dive into data-driven insights about the coffee industry, visualized through compelling charts and reports.

## 📊 Project Overview

- **Analyze** global coffee production data from 1990–2018
- **Compare** top producing nations and their market shares
- **Visualize** price distributions paid to growers
- **Explore** relationships between retail prices and consumption
- **Create** advanced meshgrid reports and subplots for deeper insights

## 🚀 Features & Visualizations

- **Bar Charts:** Top 10 coffee producers, using country abbreviations for clarity
- **Line Charts:** Production trends for top 5 nations over time, with legends and clear axes
- **Stackplots:** Brazil’s share of global production vs. rest of world, showing market growth
- **Scatterplots:** Compare Brazil vs. Vietnam and Venezuela to highlight production relationships
- **Pie & Donut Charts:** Market composition and Brazil’s dominance in 1990 and 2018
- **Dual Axis Bar Charts:** Price vs. consumption for importing nations, revealing economic patterns
- **Subplot Grids:** Distribution of prices paid to growers, comparing Brazil, Colombia, Ethiopia, and others
- **Meshgrid Report:** A single figure combining donut charts, stackplots, bar charts, and pie charts for a holistic view of Brazil’s place in the global coffee market

All visualizations are generated programmatically in the notebook, with clear titles, legends, and color schemes for professional presentation.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- Python 3
- Jupyter Notebook
- pandas
- Matplotlib
- NumPy
