# Google Play Store EDA & Feature Engineering 🎮

This project presents a comprehensive Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) and feature engineering workflow on **the Google Play Store dataset**, containing 20 columns and 10,841 rows. The analysis uncovers key trends, cleans the data, and extracts actionable insights about app popularity, installs, ratings, and more.

## 📊Project Overview

- **Objective:**  
  Analyze Google Play Store apps to identify the most popular categories, top installed apps, apps with the largest size, and those with perfect ratings.

- **Steps Followed:**  
  1. Data Cleaning  
  2. Exploratory Data Analysis  
  3. Feature Engineering

## 🧩Key Insights

- **Most Popular Category:**  
  - GAME leads with over 35 billion installs.
- **Top Apps by Category:**  
  - Subway Surfers (Game), Hangouts (Communication), Google Drive (Productivity), Instagram (Social).
- **App Ratings:**  
  - 271 apps have a perfect 5-star rating.
- **Data Cleaning:**  
  - Addressed missing values, inconsistent formats, and duplicate records.
- **Feature Engineering:**  
  - Transformed date, size, installs, and price columns for deeper analysis.

## 🧮Tech Stack

- Python
- Pandas

## 📊Sample Visualizations

- Top 10 App Categories (bar chart)
- Most Popular Categories by Installations (horizontal bar chart)

## 📝Results

- The GAME category dominates installs.
- Data cleaning and feature engineering are crucial for reliable insights.
- Visualizations reveal trends in app popularity and user ratings.
